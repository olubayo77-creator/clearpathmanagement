How rotation works

💡 The idea: Instead of paying for every streaming service every month, you rotate through them — Netflix in January, Max in February, Hulu in March — catching up on shows as you go.

🔧 Sliders: Each active service has a slider showing how much of your budget it uses. You can lower this if you're on a cheaper tier or splitting with someone.

🎬 Browse shows: Every service card has a "Browse popular shows" button. Pick the shows you want to watch and we'll add them to the right month automatically.

📅 Calendar export: Hit "Export calendar" to download a .ics file you can import into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. It includes both subscribe reminders and cancel alerts so you never overpay.

🤖 Olu AI: Use the chat box on the right to ask questions like "When should I subscribe to Max for Euphoria?" or "How do I fit Netflix and Hulu in my budget?"
